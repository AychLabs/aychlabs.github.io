Eventlight is a countdown app made by Aych Labs LLC. It is built to work entirely on your phone: there are no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and we don't run any servers that receive your countdowns. This policy explains what that means, what little information does reach us, and the choices you have.

## Information We Collect

**We do not collect any personal information through the Eventlight app.** Nothing you create in the app is sent to Aych Labs.

### Stored only on your phone

Eventlight saves the following in its private storage on your device. It never leaves your phone except through your own Google backup (see "Backups to Your Google Account" below).

| Data | What it's used for |
|---|---|
| Countdowns: title, date, time, time zone, category, repeat rule, "time since" wording and reminder choices | Showing your countdowns in the app and on widgets |
| Settings: theme, custom colors, "Show seconds on widgets" and default reminders | Remembering how you like the app to look and behave |
| Background photos you choose | Eventlight keeps its own resized copy (about 2048 pixels on the long side) plus crop positions for each widget size. Your original photo is not changed. |
| Widget setups | Remembering which countdowns each home-screen widget shows |
| Unlock status | A saved copy of whether you've bought the unlock, so widgets can show the right content without asking Google Play each time |

Eventlight only receives the photos you pick for a background. It does not scan, read or upload the rest of your photo library.

### Information Google Play provides to us

- **Purchases.** If you buy the Eventlight unlock, Google Play handles the payment. Google gives us limited order information (for example an order number, the date, the product and the price). We never see your card or bank details.
- **Crash and performance reports.** If you've allowed your phone to share usage and diagnostics data with Google, Google Play gives developers crash reports and performance statistics (for example the device model, Android version, app version and technical details of the error). These reports do not include your countdowns and do not identify you to us.
- **Store statistics and reviews.** Google Play shows us overall numbers such as installs and ratings, and any public review you choose to post.

### Information you send us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message.

## How We Use Information

- **Data on your phone** is used only by the app, on your phone, to show your countdowns, widgets and reminders.
- **Order information** is used to answer questions about purchases, handle refunds and keep the business and tax records the law requires.
- **Crash and performance reports** are used to find and fix bugs.
- **Emails** are used to reply to you.

We do not use any information for advertising, profiling or tracking. Eventlight contains no ads.

## How We Share Information

**We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.** Because your countdowns, photos and settings never reach us, we have nothing of yours from the app to share.

We may disclose order records or emails only if the law requires it, to protect our legal rights, or as part of a sale or merger of Aych Labs LLC (in which case this policy would continue to apply to that information).

## Backups to Your Google Account

If backup is turned on for your phone, Android automatically copies Eventlight's data (countdowns, settings and background photos, up to about 20 MB of photos) to your Google account, so your countdowns come back when you set up a new phone. Samsung Smart Switch can also carry this data to a new phone.

This backup is handled by Google and stored in your Google account under Google's privacy policy. Aych Labs cannot see or access it. Widget setups and temporary image files are not backed up.

You can turn backup off in your phone's settings (on most phones: **Settings → Google → Backup**; the exact location varies by manufacturer).

## Permissions

Eventlight asks for permissions only when a feature needs them, and every feature still works (in a simpler form) if you say no.

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Alarms & reminders | Lets widgets roll over exactly on time and show live seconds. Without it, widgets show days instead ("31 days", "Tomorrow", "Today"). |
| Notifications | Sends the optional reminders you turn on (1 month, 1 week and 1 day before an event). Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no notification server is involved. |
| Run at startup | Restarts widget updates after your phone restarts. |
| Google Play billing | Lets you buy and restore the one-time unlock through Google Play. |

Eventlight does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone or advertising ID.

## Third-Party Services

Eventlight contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting SDKs (for example, no Firebase). The only outside services involved are:

| Service | Provider | What it receives |
|---|---|---|
| Google Play (app distribution, billing, crash reports) | Google LLC | Your purchase and payment details, which you give directly to Google; crash and performance data if you've allowed your phone to share diagnostics |
| Android Backup Service | Google LLC | A copy of Eventlight's data in your Google account, if backup is on |
| Zoho Mail | Zoho Corporation | Emails you send to an @aychlabs.com address |

Google's privacy policy is at [policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). Zoho's privacy policy is at [zoho.com/privacy.html](https://www.zoho.com/privacy.html).

## Data Retention

- **Data on your phone** stays until you delete it in the app, clear Eventlight's storage, or uninstall the app. Uninstalling removes all of Eventlight's data from your phone.
- **Your Google backup** is kept and deleted by Google according to Google's backup rules. You can delete it from your Google account at any time.
- **Order records** are kept for 7 years after the purchase, to meet tax and accounting requirements.
- **Emails** are kept for 2 years after our last message with you, then deleted. You can ask us to delete them sooner.
- **Crash and performance reports** are kept by Google in Google Play Console according to Google's retention schedule.

## Data Security

Eventlight's data lives in the app's private storage, which Android keeps separate from other apps, and it is protected by your phone's screen lock. The app saves changes in a way that prevents data from being corrupted if your phone shuts off mid-save. Google encrypts backups stored in your Google account.

No system is perfectly secure, so we recommend keeping a screen lock on your phone.

## Your Rights and Choices

- **View, change or delete your countdowns** at any time in the app.
- **Delete all of Eventlight's data** by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. Step-by-step instructions are on our [Delete your Eventlight data](/eventlight/delete-account/) page.
- **Turn off Google backup** in your phone's settings.
- **Say no to permissions**, or change them later in your phone's settings. The app keeps working.
- **Ask what we hold about you.** Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR or California's CCPA give you rights to access, correct or delete personal information. Since the app sends us nothing, the only information we could hold about you is an email you sent us or a purchase record from Google Play. Email us and we'll tell you what we have and delete what we're allowed to.

## Children's Privacy

Eventlight is a general-audience app for people 13 and older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The app itself collects no personal information from anyone. If you believe a child under 13 has sent us personal information by email, contact us and we will delete it.

## Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. If we ever add a feature that changes how data is handled (for example, syncing countdowns between devices), we will update this policy before that feature is released and point out the change in the app or its store listing.

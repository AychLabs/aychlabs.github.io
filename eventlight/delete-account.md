This page explains how to delete your Eventlight data. Eventlight is developed by Aych Labs LLC. Eventlight has no user accounts and no servers that store your countdowns: everything lives on your phone, so deleting your data happens on your phone.

> **Important:** Deleting Eventlight's data does not cancel or remove your unlock purchase. Your purchase belongs to your Google account, and you can restore it anytime with **Restore purchase** in Eventlight's Settings.

## Delete Your Entire Account

Because there is no Eventlight account, deleting all of Eventlight's data from your phone is the same as deleting your account.

### In the app

Eventlight's data is removed using your phone's settings:

1. Open your phone's **Settings** app.
2. Tap **Apps**, then **Eventlight**.
3. Tap **Storage**, then **Clear data** (on some phones, **Clear storage**), and confirm.
4. Optional: tap **Uninstall** to remove the app as well. Uninstalling also deletes all of Eventlight's data.
5. If you have Eventlight widgets on your home screen, press and hold each one and tap **Remove**.

To also remove the copy in your Google backup, delete your phone's backup from your Google account (in the Google One app or at [one.google.com/storage](https://one.google.com/storage), under **Backups**), or turn backup off in **Settings → Google → Backup**. The exact menu names vary by phone manufacturer.

### By email

Aych Labs does not hold your countdowns, so there is nothing of that kind for us to delete. We may hold emails you sent us and records of your purchase. To have those deleted:

- **Email:** [privacy@aychlabs.com](mailto:privacy@aychlabs.com)
- **Subject line:** `Eventlight Data Deletion Request`
- **Include:** the email address you contacted us from (if any) and, if you bought the unlock, your Google Play order number (it starts with "GPA." and is in your Google Play purchase receipt email).

## Delete Specific Data Only

You can delete individual pieces of data without clearing everything:

- **A countdown:** open the countdown, tap **Edit**, then tap **Delete countdown**. This also deletes any background photo copy saved for that countdown.
- **A background photo:** open the countdown, tap **Edit**, and choose one of the theme backgrounds instead of the photo. Eventlight deletes its copy of the photo.
- **A widget's setup:** press and hold the widget on your home screen and tap **Remove**. Eventlight deletes that widget's settings.
- **Reminders:** turn off reminders for a countdown in its **Edit** screen.
- **Emails or purchase records we hold:** email [privacy@aychlabs.com](mailto:privacy@aychlabs.com) with the subject line `Partial Data Deletion Request` and say which data you want deleted.

## What Is Deleted

When you clear Eventlight's storage or uninstall the app, the following are permanently deleted from your phone:

- All countdowns and their settings (titles, dates, times, categories, repeat rules, reminders and "time since" wording)
- App settings and custom theme colors
- Eventlight's copies of your background photos (your original photos in your gallery are not affected)
- Widget setups
- The saved copy of your unlock status

When you email a deletion request, we delete your emails and any copies of purchase records we're allowed to delete.

## What Is Retained

| Item | Why it's kept | How long |
|---|---|---|
| Your unlock purchase | It belongs to your Google account so you can restore it on any phone. It is held by Google Play, not by Aych Labs. | As long as Google keeps your purchase history |
| Your Google backup of Eventlight's data | It's stored in your Google account, which Aych Labs cannot access | Until you delete it, or until Google removes it under its backup rules |
| Order records | Required for tax and accounting | 7 years after the purchase |

Nothing else is retained.

## How Long It Takes

- **On your phone:** clearing storage, uninstalling, or deleting a countdown takes effect immediately.
- **Email requests:** we confirm your request within 7 days and complete it within 30 days.

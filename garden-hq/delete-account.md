This page explains how to delete your Garden HQ account and data. Garden HQ is developed by Aych Labs. You can ask us to delete your account, or just some of your data, at any time, and you don't need the app installed to do it.

## Delete Your Entire Account

### In the app

1. Open Garden HQ and tap the **Settings** tab.
2. Under **Garden HQ**, tap **Request account deletion**.
3. Tap **Email a deletion request**. Your email app opens a message to support@aychlabs.com with the subject "Garden HQ Account Deletion Request" and your account ID already filled in.
4. Send the email.

### By email

If you no longer have the app, email **support@aychlabs.com** with the subject line:

**Garden HQ Account Deletion Request**

Include one of these so we can find your account:

- **Your account ID.** Garden HQ shows it under **Settings → Request account deletion**, as "Your account ID".
- **The Google email address you used for backup** (if you used **Back up with Google** or **Restore from Google**). Please send the email from that address, so we know the account is yours.

If we can't match your request to an account, we'll reply and ask for your account ID or for an email from your backup address.

> **Important:** We will never ask for your password.

## Delete Specific Data Only

You can delete some data without closing your account.

**In the app:**

- **Photos:** in the **Plants** tab, open the plant, tap the photo, tap **Delete**, then tap **Delete** again to confirm. This removes the photo from your phone and its record (caption and date taken) from our database.

**By email:** for anything else, email **support@aychlabs.com** with the subject line **Partial Data Deletion Request**. Tell us what to delete (for example a garden by name, a plant in a garden, a custom plant, or a plant's care history) and include your account ID or send it from your Google backup address, as above. Garden HQ doesn't yet have delete buttons for gardens, plants, custom plants or care history, so we remove those for you on request. Marking a plant **Harvested**, **Removed** or **Didn't make it** hides it from your garden but doesn't delete it.

## What Is Deleted

When we delete your account, we delete:

- Your account: its account ID and, if you backed up with Google, its link to your Google account (your Google email address and name).
- All your gardens and everything in them: names, ZIP codes and zones, plants, reminders, care history, and photo records (captions and dates).
- Your custom plants.

Your photos themselves are stored only on your phone and were never uploaded to us. To remove them, uninstall Garden HQ or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

Deleting your account can't be undone.

## What Is Retained

- **A record of your request:** the date of your request and your account ID, without any of your garden data. We keep it for **12 months** to show that the request was fulfilled, then delete it.
- **Database backups:** our database's automatic backups expire within **7 days**, so deleted data is gone from them no later than 7 days after we delete it.

Nothing else is kept.

## How Long It Takes

We delete your account or the data you asked about within **30 days** of your request and email you to confirm. Copies in our automatic backups expire within 7 days after that.

DATA DELETION

Delete Your Account & Data

You have the right to permanently delete your Family Rewards account and all associated data at any time. This page explains how to do it and exactly what gets removed.

Completed within 30 days of request

Option 1 — Delete directly in the app

1

Open Family Rewards

Launch the Family Rewards app on your Android device and make sure you're signed in to the account you want to delete.

2

Go to Settings

Tap the ⚙ Settings icon in the top-right corner of the main screen (or navigate to Menu → Account Settings).

3

Select "Delete Account"

Scroll to the bottom of the Account Settings screen and tap Delete Account. You'll be shown a summary of what will be deleted.

4

Confirm deletion

Type DELETE to confirm, then tap the red Permanently Delete button. Your request is submitted immediately. You'll receive a confirmation email within 24 hours.

Option 2 — Request by email

If you no longer have access to the app or prefer to submit your request in writing, email us at privacy@aychlabs.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

Please include the email address associated with your Family Rewards account. We will verify your identity and process the deletion within 30 days. We'll send a confirmation once complete.

30

Days to complete deletion

All personal data will be permanently deleted or anonymized within 30 days of your confirmed request. You'll receive an email confirmation when the process is complete. Crash log data (anonymized, no personal info) is retained for up to 90 days per our standard rotation schedule.

What gets deleted vs. retained

Permanently Deleted

  • Parent account email address
  • Hashed password and auth credentials
  • All child/family member names
  • All task and chore records
  • All points history and balances
  • All reward redemption records
  • All task completion photos
  • All account settings and preferences
  • Firebase Auth account entry
  • All Firestore documents for the account

Temporarily Retained

  • Anonymized crash logs (no personal info) — deleted within 90 days per standard rotation
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable usage stats — permanently anonymized, never linked to your account
  • Transaction records if required by tax law — retained only as legally required, then deleted

We do not sell data. Retained items contain no personal information linked to you or your children.

Account & Data Deletion

You have the right to delete your entire Family Rewards account, or to request deletion of specific data only — without closing your account. This page covers both options.

All requests completed within 30 days
Delete entire account
or
Delete specific data only
Delete Entire Account
Permanently closes your account and removes all associated data

Delete directly in the app

1

Open Family Rewards

Launch the Family Rewards app on your Android device and make sure you're signed in to the account you want to delete.

2

Go to Settings

Tap the ⚙ Settings icon in the top-right corner of the main screen (or navigate to Menu → Account Settings).

3

Select "Delete Account"

Scroll to the bottom of Account Settings and tap Delete Account. You'll be shown a summary of what will be deleted.

4

Confirm deletion

Type DELETE to confirm, then tap the red Permanently Delete button. Your request is submitted immediately. You'll receive a confirmation email within 24 hours.

Request by email

If you no longer have access to the app or prefer to submit your request in writing, email us at privacy@aychlabs.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request."

Include the email address on your Family Rewards account. We'll verify your identity and complete the deletion within 30 days, then send a confirmation.

30

Days to complete full deletion

All personal data will be permanently deleted or anonymized within 30 days of your confirmed request. Crash log data (anonymized, no personal info) cycles out within 90 days per our standard rotation.

What gets deleted vs. retained

Permanently Deleted

  • Parent account email address
  • Hashed password and auth credentials
  • All child/family member names
  • All task and chore records
  • All points history and balances
  • All reward redemption records
  • All task completion photos
  • All account settings and preferences
  • Firebase Auth account entry
  • All Firestore documents for the account

Temporarily Retained

  • Anonymized crash logs (no personal info) — deleted within 90 days per standard rotation
  • Aggregated, non-identifiable usage stats — permanently anonymized, never linked to your account
  • Transaction records if required by tax law — retained only as legally required, then deleted

We do not sell data. Retained items contain no personal information linked to you or your children.

Delete Specific Data Only
Keep your account open while removing particular records or resetting family data

You don't have to close your account to delete data. You can request removal of specific records — such as a child's profile, task history, photos, or all family data — while keeping your parent account intact.

Reset Family Data

Wipes all task history, points balances, and reward records for your entire family — your account, family member profiles, and settings remain. This mirrors the Reset Family Data feature inside the app.

Remove a Child Profile

Deletes a specific family member's name, task records, points history, and any associated photos. Your parent account and other family members are not affected.

Delete Task Completion Photos

Removes some or all stored photos without affecting task records, points, or any other data. Photos can also be deleted individually within the app.

Other Specific Data Request

Have a different request? Describe what you'd like removed and we'll work with you. We honor all valid data deletion rights under COPPA, CCPA, and GDPR.

How to submit a partial data deletion request

Email privacy@aychlabs.com with the subject line "Partial Data Deletion Request" and include:

  • The email address on your Family Rewards account
  • What specific data you want deleted (e.g. "all photos," "my child Maya's profile," "task history before June 2025")
  • Whether you want to keep your account open after the deletion

We'll verify your identity and confirm the deletion within 30 days. We will never delete more than you request without your explicit confirmation.

30

Days to complete partial deletion

Specific data removal requests are processed within 30 days. Your account remains fully active throughout. You'll receive an email confirmation once the requested data has been removed.
